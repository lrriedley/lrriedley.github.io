Dear Hiring Team,

I've built a consumer ordering app and I've run a restaurant floor, and I want to do the first job with the second one in my back pocket. I'm writing to apply for the Product Manager role on your team.

On the tech product side, I've owned product for Night District, a nightlife technology company. I built the guest app in Swift and SwiftUI myself, ran the sprint backlog, and set the product vision end to end. When we couldn't achieve product-market fit, we decided to pivot.

Night District now runs a network of nightlife vending machines in Louisville and Southern Indiana. Since the pivot, I've worked directly with bar general managers and owners to earn our venue placements. Through a thorough understanding of the bar operator's and customer's needs, Night District is now the largest nightlife vending machine operator in the city with monthly revenue exceededing $7,500.

On the hospitality management front, I am the General Manager of the Black Rabbit Speakeasy, where I planned and implemented a 6-cocktail beverage program, including recipes, bartender training, and quality standards. Before that I led a Navy culinary team feeding 250-plus people a day, and I bartended at a resort property. I know what an operator needs from a product because I've been the one checking sales, training the team, and fielding guest complaints at close. That's the empathy a role like this needs for interviews, usability testing, and customer feedback to turn into real product decisions, not guesses.

I'm CAPM-certified and Scrum-trained, and I'm finishing a CIS degree at the University of Louisville with a concentration in business process management. I'd welcome the chance to talk about where your product goes next.
